# Editorial Policy

## Pillars

- `motivational_inspirational`
- `personal_journey_recovery`
- `artistic_process`
- `explanation_education`
- `humor_personality`
- `mistakes_problem_solving`
- `emergent_miscellaneous`

Each candidate has exactly one primary pillar and may have secondary pillars.

## Selection Rules

Favor segments with a hook, visible action, Nate or hands in frame, clear artwork views, intelligible speech, a distinct story or demonstration, honest standalone context, and potential for one or more shorts.

Penalize dead air, administrative chatter, repetitive greetings, poor visuals, unclear audio, excessive context requirements, repetition, and misleading extraction risk.

Personal journey and recovery clips should preserve enough context to avoid misrepresentation.
